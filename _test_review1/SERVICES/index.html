    CHERENKOV — Expert Services

    AI-native quality engineering, led by a QA lead who's done it in fintech and telecom. The fastest path to revenue while the open-source engine builds adoption — and the funnel that turns services clients into design partners for the commercial tier. See Brand & Strategy §5/§8.

    Who this is for

    Engineering and QA leaders at API-first companies (50–500 eng) — especially fintech, telecom, health, and other regulated/privacy-sensitive orgs — who:

    • ship faster than their test coverage can keep up, and suspect their specs and services have silently drifted apart,
    • want to adopt AI in testing without sending source code or traffic to a third-party cloud,
    • need an automation strategy that survives spec changes instead of breaking on them.

    Why me: Moayed Badawy — QA Lead (Celfocus; prev. Nomo Fintech [London], Capiter, Eventtus). 8 years, test-engineer → lead. Built and led a 13-person QA team, cut manual testing 40% with Selenium/Java/JMeter/Appium, and ran API testing across fintech and data-science products. ISTQB CTFL + ICAgile. I build the tools I wished existed — CHERENKOV is one of them.

    Offers

    1. Conformance Audit — fixed fee, 1–2 weeks

    Point CHERENKOV at your real services (spec + live/staging traffic). I deliver a report of real, reproduced spec↔implementation divergences your team missed, ranked by user/risk impact, each with a runnable Playwright test. Local-first — nothing leaves your environment. Deliverable: divergence report + ejected test suite + a 60-min readout. Outcome: you see exactly where your software lies.

    2. AI-in-Testing Strategy — fixed fee, 2–4 weeks

    A pragmatic roadmap to introduce local LLM / AI-native testing into your SDLC without the privacy, cost, or lock-in traps. Tool selection, model/substrate choice, CI integration, coverage targets, and a 90-day adoption plan tailored to your stack and compliance posture. Deliverable: strategy doc + reference CI pipeline + team enablement session.

    3. Quality Engineering Retainer — monthly

    Ongoing, fractional Head-of-Quality engagement: automation architecture, CHERENKOV deployment (incl. air-gapped/K8s), QMS and release-risk processes, and team mentoring. Built for orgs that need senior quality leadership without a full-time hire.

    4. Design-Partner Program — discounted / co-development

    Get early access to the CHERENKOV Team/Enterprise tier (dashboard, CI orchestration, K8s operator, RBAC/audit) in exchange for feedback and a case study. Preferential pricing locked in at GA.

    How services feed the product

    Audit  →  finds real divergences  →  proves value in days
     ↘  client becomes a Design Partner  →  shapes the Team/Enterprise tier
          ↘  converts to a paid commercial account at GA  →  recurring revenue

    Every engagement also produces a public artifact (anonymized divergence write-up / talk) that feeds the open-source funnel and your personal brand.

    Engagement

    • Local-first by default. Audits run inside your environment; your specs, code, and traffic stay yours.
    • No lock-in. All generated tests eject to vanilla Playwright and run forever without CHERENKOV.

# E0.6 — Verification of external-analysis claims

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · **Method:** 12 independent agents, one per contested claim, each reading the actual code and required to cite `file:line`. 13 agents total including cross-synthesis. Measured at `feat/qa-headless-locator-alignment`, HEAD `404ffb2`.

## Why this exists

Roughly ten external AI analyses of CHERENKOV-QA were commissioned. They contradicted each other on load-bearing facts — whether the AST claim is real, how large the codebase is, whether the dashboard works, whether the tool serves manual QAs. Roadmap decisions rested on those facts, so they were checked rather than averaged.

**The corpus has a systematic flaw.** Every source is an AI analyst reading the same repository and README. That resembles triangulation but is **correlated error** — ten readers of one artifact produce one opinion with ten signatures. The distinction matters most where the analyses were unanimous:

| Claim | Sources | Real confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Test-integrity auditing is novel vs. competitors | 10/10 | **High** — checkable against competitor feature sets |
| Market timing is excellent | 8/10 | **Low** — no analyst observed a buyer |
| Manual QAs need this | 7/10 | **Low** — no manual QA was consulted |

Novelty is verifiable from documents. **Demand is not, and it is what every proposed roadmap assumes.** E0.3 remains the only instrument in this programme that produces demand evidence.

One later analysis claimed to "verify" the AST question with an evidence column quoting `README.md`. Quoting the marketing copy is not verification of the marketing copy.

## The one that was flatly FALSE

MEISSNER / ABLATION / TOKAMAK, Qdrant, LATTICE, shred receipts, WAL/WORM, "Trident of Truth", "5 of 20 validated scanners", cryptographic proof chains, air-gapped sovereign AI — **none exist in this repository.** A case-insensitive `git grep` over all tracked files returns two non-code matches.

They describe **`cherenkov-professional`** — a separate repo with a separate remote (`github.com/moaidmoatasem/cherenkov-professional`), present locally at `/home/moaid/cherenkov-professional`.

Roughly two full analyses in the corpus are about a different codebase. Treat any analysis containing those terms as contaminated and quarantine it whole — do not attempt to merge the security-platform framing into this roadmap.

## The one that was flatly TRUE

Both rate limiters exist, share a class name, and are both registered — confirmed empirically at runtime, not just by reading. `api.py:53` registers the token-bucket `RateLimitMiddleware`; `api.py:124` → `security.py:125` registers a different class of the same name (sliding window). Sustained limits differ 6× (1.67 vs 10 rps), burst 5× (20 vs 100), and they return two different 429 body schemas — only one carrying `Retry-After`. `CORSMiddleware` is innermost, so neither 429 carries CORS headers and the React UI surfaces rate-limiting as an opaque CORS failure. ~10 lines in one file.

## What none of the ten analyses noticed

1. **`gate="ast"` is a structured output field, not prose.** Every analysis that raised the AST issue framed it as a README/marketing problem. It is worse: `review.py:183` writes `gate="ast"` into a machine-readable `GateResult` consumed downstream and rendered in reports. The product names a regex check "ast" in its own data.
2. **The Allure exporter fabricates passing results** — and `tests/standalone/test_allure_emitter.py:31-32` asserts the fabricated name, locking the behaviour in. For a product whose pitch is catching fabricated test results, this is the sharpest self-inflicted wound in the tree, and no analyst found it.
3. **TypeScript HALLUCINATED detection is `pass`.** Not weak — absent. And `.ts` silently skips the honest "regex-based detection" warning at `check_suite.py:226` because `.ts` sits inside the guard tuple. The correct sentence already exists in the codebase and is shown to nobody who needs it.
4. **`synthesize_mutant_battery` has zero production callers.** Written and unit-tested during the E0.5f work; `review.py:397-418` still uses the single coarse mutant that `docs/evidence/e0.5e_oracle_discrimination.md` measured at **0/3** cheat-class detection. The fix for the known-weak gate is already written and simply not wired.
5. **`cherenkov author` exists** (`epoch.py:67-76`, registered `core.py:33,85`) but the web seam discards the user's `intent`. Analysts recommended building it; it needs connecting, not building.
6. **`bedrock.py` and `azure_openai.py` have zero importers, zero tests, and reference settings keys that do not exist.** Delete rather than complete.

## Sequencing consequence

Every external roadmap put distribution first. The verification inverts that. Five Tier-0 defects are falsifiable by a hostile reader in under a minute, and each is the product failing at precisely what it sells. Publishing to PyPI first converts private bugs into a public, permanent credibility event for a tool whose entire positioning is trust.

**Fix Tier 0, then distribute.** Tracked as M0b in `docs/ROADMAP_2026H2.md`.
